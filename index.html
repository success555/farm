About Doutare Agro Farm Limited • RC: 9563075

Rooted in Passion, Driven by Purpose

Doutare Agro Farm Limited (RC: 9563075) is a Nigerian agribusiness company focused on sustainable crop production, livestock, and agro-processing. We specialize in high-yield Plantain, cassava, maize, poultry, and fish farming, with a commitment to food security and youth empowerment in our host communities.

We recognize that sustainable development in Nigeria begins with food security and local economic vibrancy. By focusing on essential staples and protein sources—namely Plantain, Cassava, Maize, Poultry, and Fish farming—we satisfy market demand while creating lasting social value.

1. Grow Food

Combating hunger and strengthening national food security through high-yield cultivation, clean aquaculture, and biosecure livestock production.

2. Grow People

Empowering rural youths and host community members in Ayakoromo and Burutu LGA through practical farming techniques, mentorship, and stable employment.

3. Grow Wealth

Unlocking the commercial power of agro-processing, value addition, and bulk distribution to foster sustainable agrarian wealth for our region.
